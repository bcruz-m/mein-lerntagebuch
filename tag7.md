# Pack & Go – Building a First Prototype

## What I learned
I learned how to turn one part of a larger product idea into a small, functional prototype.

Instead of trying to build my complete styling app, I focused on one feature: Pack & Go, a travel wardrobe planner.

The prototype lets users select the trip duration, expected weather and travel occasion. It then generates a packing list, allows users to adjust quantities, mark items as packed and track their progress.

## Problem
My original styling app is much larger and includes several features. Building everything at once would have made the first prototype too complex.

I also initially built the prototype with Codex, while the exercise was demonstrated using Claude Code.

## How I solved it
I reduced the scope to one practical use case and used simple predefined rules instead of AI recommendations or live weather data.

I tested the prototype on desktop and mobile and published it on Netlify so it could be accessed through a public URL.

This helped me understand how to turn a larger product vision into a small MVP that can be built and tested quickly.
