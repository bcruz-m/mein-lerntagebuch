# From Assumptions to a UX Briefing

## What I learned
I learned how to turn user research into more structured product decisions.

Using the findings from my styling app research, I worked on the ICP, critical assumptions, risks and a UX Design Briefing.

## Problem
Some assumptions about the product could easily be mistaken for validated facts.

For example, features may sound attractive, but that does not automatically mean users are willing to invest the time required to use them or trust AI-generated recommendations.

## How I solved it
I reviewed the available research and distinguished between what had already been supported by user feedback and what was still uncertain.

I documented the remaining risks instead of treating them as validated.

This helped me understand how research, assumptions and risks influence the design of an MVP.
