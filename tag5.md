# User Research & Product Validation

## What I learned
For my final project, I started exploring the idea of an AI-assisted styling and wardrobe app.

Instead of immediately building the application, I learned to first investigate the problem and the potential users.

I conducted interviews and collected responses through a questionnaire. I then organized the findings and used them to refine my Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

## Problem
My initial product idea included many assumptions about what users needed and which features they would actually use.

Some ideas that sounded useful to me were not yet supported by evidence from users.

## How I solved it
I compared the interview and survey responses, looked for recurring problems and separated observations from assumptions.

This helped me narrow the target group and identify which parts of the product idea were supported by research and which still needed validation.

I learned that product decisions should be based on evidence rather than only on my own assumptions.
